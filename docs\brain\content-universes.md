# Content Universes

Content universes are creative territories, not permanent audience silos. Concepts may cross universes, and the portfolio should rotate among them so the brand never becomes trapped inside one demographic.

| Universe | Productive territory | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Aging | dignity decay, changing bodies, accumulated audacity | No “old people are useless” shorthand |
| Women | social expectations, rage, friendship, contradiction | Women are not a monolith |
| Men | masculinity, confidence, habits, emotional evasions | Mock behavior and scripts, not inherent worth |
| LGBTQ+ | community life, dating, chosen family, cultural specificity | No anti-LGBTQ attacks or outsider contempt |
| Relationships | intimacy, resentment, negotiation, domestic weirdness | Avoid generic partner-bashing |
| Dating | apps, performance, red flags, optimism under pressure | Specificity over “dating sucks” |
| Marriage | logistics, familiarity, teamwork, petty warfare | No tired “spouse bad” defaults |
| Divorce | reinvention, paperwork, freedom, absurd aftermath | Avoid flattening real harm into cheap slogans |
| Sex | desire, awkwardness, communication, bodies, confidence | Adult and consensual; never exploitative |
| Pets | projection, devotion, animal chaos, household power | The pet must do more than simply be cute |
| Work | meetings, management theatre, burnout, office language | Avoid stale Monday/coffee material |
| Family | inherited weirdness, obligations, group dynamics | No punching down at children or dependents |
| Introvert / antisocial | avoidance, depleted batteries, strategic disappearance | Avoid generic “I hate people” merchandise copy |
| Generational | competing norms, technology, language, selective memory | No generation is a single personality |
| Menopause | heat, sleep, rage, medicine, invisibility, freedom | Treat lived experience as specific, not a costume |
| Drinking / partying / bad decisions | confidence, aftermath, lore, questionable judgment | Do not glamorize dependency or coercion |
| Everyday WTF | errands, systems, public behavior, tiny humiliations | Observation must be more specific than annoyance |
| Experimental / unhinged | surreal premises, risky formats, strange characters | Weirdness still needs an intelligible comic engine |

## Portfolio rules

- Track primary and secondary universes per concept.
- Review the recent mix before generating another batch.
- Treat repeated dominance by one universe as a portfolio warning, even when that universe performs well.
- Explore underserved universes through experiments rather than forcing quotas into every batch.
- Separate universe fit from target audience; a menopause concept may appeal beyond people experiencing menopause.
