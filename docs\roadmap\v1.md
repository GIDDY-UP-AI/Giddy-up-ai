# V1 Roadmap

## Outcome

Give Markie a reliable queue of fewer, stronger Giddy Up concepts and make every decision improve the next batch.

## Loop

### 1. Research

Capture observations, tensions, language, format patterns, and source provenance across multiple content universes. Research produces ingredients, not copied jokes.

**Exit:** a structured research brief with usable observations and no unresolved rights or safety concern.

### 2. Generate

Turn research into multiple premises and variants using different comic mechanisms. Maintain universe and audience breadth.

**Exit:** typed draft concepts with explicit hook, comic engine, audience hypothesis, and format hypothesis.

### 3. Critique

Apply brand rules, safety/target checks, cliché detection, AI-tell detection, and the internal scorecard. Record the rationale.

**Exit:** weak concepts rejected; salvageable concepts routed to revision; passing concepts eligible for ranking.

### 4. Rank

Rank passing work by quality, learned user taste, strategic fit, batch diversity, and experiments. Keep adjustments explainable.

**Exit:** a small ordered shortlist with creative briefs and visible reasons.

### 5. Approve / Reject

Markie chooses `LOVE`, `LIKE`, `MEH`, `HATE`, or `NEVER_AGAIN`, adds optional reasons, and separately approves, rejects, or requests revision.

**Exit:** an auditable decision; only approved work may enter Production.

### 6. Learn

Store raw feedback, derive tentative preferences, enforce explicit `NEVER_AGAIN` rules, and show how new evidence affects future ranking. Later, ingest published performance as a separate lane.

**Exit:** updated, evidence-linked preferences without rewriting raw history.

## Foundation milestones

1. **Repository foundation:** brand/agent/architecture specifications, typed model, scoring engine, local prototype, tests.
2. **Creative workbench:** research briefs, generation batches, critique UI, shortlist controls.
3. **Markie review loop:** durable feedback reasons, approval history, preference evidence, revision routing.
4. **Production handoff:** approved-concept briefs and asset review states, still without auto-publishing.
5. **Performance recording:** manual/imported observations, normalization rules, separate audience learning.

## V1 non-goals

- e-commerce or product development;
- autonomous publishing or social credentials;
- the Markie avatar;
- expensive infrastructure;
- opaque autonomous learning;
- any Figgy / Go Fig integration.

## Success criteria

- Markie sees only concepts that pass internal gates.
- Every shown concept has a clear premise, score, critique, and ranking rationale.
- Feedback takes seconds but can capture rich reasons.
- `NEVER_AGAIN` patterns are enforceable and reversible.
- User taste and audience performance remain independently inspectable.
- The content portfolio remains broad across universes.
- The system can replace local persistence later without rewriting domain logic.
