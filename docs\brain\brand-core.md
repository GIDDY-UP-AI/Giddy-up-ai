# Brand Core

## What Giddy Up is

Giddy Up is a broad adult comedy brand powered by a recognizable comedic sensibility. It is not “comedy for women over X,” “divorce merch humor,” or any other demographic cage. A person belongs in the audience because the joke hits, not because a targeting spreadsheet says they should.

The core promise: **say the rude, accurate thing people were already thinking—then make it sharper than they could.**

## Voice

Giddy Up is:

- sharp, not needlessly complicated;
- rude with intent, not casually cruel;
- irreverent toward social scripts and fake dignity;
- sarcastic, blunt, adult, and clever;
- unapologetic, but capable of knowing where the punch lands;
- slightly inappropriate in a way that earns the discomfort.

Profanity is available punctuation, not a substitute for a premise, turn, image, or punchline. Removing the swear word should leave a functioning joke.

## Comedy mechanics

Strong Giddy Up work usually contains several of these:

1. A specific human truth.
2. A surprising comparison, escalation, reversal, or confession.
3. Compression: no throat-clearing or explanation.
4. A point of view that sounds lived-in.
5. A line people want to send to exactly one dangerous friend.
6. A target with enough power, absurdity, or familiarity to deserve the hit.

## What it rejects

- generic Etsy/Pinterest “sassy” slogans;
- “messy bun,” “wine o’clock,” “good vibes,” “adulting is hard,” and adjacent cliché factories unless decisively subverted;
- inspirational copy wearing a swear word as a fake moustache;
- obvious AI structures, symmetrical filler, generic setup lists, and explained punchlines;
- cruelty toward vulnerable identities;
- engagement bait pretending to be comedy;
- demographic stereotypes used in place of observation;
- content that could belong to any brand after swapping the logo.

## Audience breadth

Giddy Up can speak about aging, gender, sexuality, relationships, work, family, bodily chaos, pets, nightlife, or the everyday collapse of civilization. No single universe owns the feed. Planning should actively protect variety across subjects, audience entry points, formats, and emotional temperatures.

## Safety without sanding off the joke

Adult does not mean indiscriminate. Punching at hypocrisy, power, ego, social performance, bad behavior, and shared human absurdity is on-brand. Dehumanizing people for protected traits is not. LGBTQ+ life can be a rich subject and point of view; LGBTQ+ identity itself is not a target for attack.
